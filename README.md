## 📖 What Is This App?

Have you ever sat down to work or study, felt overwhelmed, and found yourself scrolling on your phone 20 minutes later? 

**Pomodoro — Make Time Count** solves that. It is a distraction-free, beautifully designed study companion based on the world-renowned **Pomodoro Technique**:
- You pick a task.
- You focus for a short sprint (traditionally 25 minutes, or whatever duration fits your workflow).
- You take a refreshing 5-minute break.
- After four sessions, you take a longer, restorative rest.

This simple rhythm prevents mental burnout, builds deep momentum, and turns intimidating study goals into achievable daily steps.

## ✨ Features You'll Love

### 1. ⏱️ Flexible Study Presets (15m to 90m+)
Not all study sessions are created equal. Select the rhythm that fits your work with a single click:
- **15m Quick Sprint** — Overcome procrastination and get into the zone.
- **25m Classic Pomodoro** — The gold standard for homework and coding.
- **45m Academic Block** — Simulates standard school and university lecture periods.
- **50m (50/10 Rule)** — Ideal for software engineering, deep writing, and project work.
- **60m Power Hour** — Sustained, uninterrupted intensive concentration.
- **90m Ultradian Flow** — Aligned with human biological focus cycles for peak performance.
- **Custom Timer (1 to 180 min)** — Set any custom duration you like in Settings!

### 2. 🎧 Ambient Study Audio (100% Offline)
Block out household distractions, noisy roommates, or coffee shop chatter:
- **🌧️ Gentle Rain** — Soft, rhythmic raindrops for calming background noise.
- **🌊 Ocean Waves** — Natural, periodic rolling swells to quiet the mind.
- **🍃 Forest Breeze** — Gentle rustling wind that soothes anxiety.
- **💨 Pink Noise** — Balanced acoustic frequency proven to improve memory and focus.
- *Includes an inline volume slider and works completely offline without downloading audio files!*

### 3. 🎯 Focus Task Tracker & Celebration Confetti
Stay intentional with the inline task bar:
- Type in what you're working on: *"Finish Chapter 4 Biology Notes"*.
- When you finish, click the checkmark or press **Enter**.
- Watch a **vibrant burst of confetti** celebrate your victory and track your total completed tasks for the day!

### 4. 🧘 Zen Fullscreen Mode
Need zero distractions? Tap **`F`** or click the expand icon in the header. The app transforms into a pure, clean digital desk clock showing only your countdown and active task.

### 5. 🌓 Warm Aesthetic Design (Light & Dark Themes)
- **Light Theme**: Warm organic paper tones (`#F8F7F4`) that reduce eye fatigue during daylight study.
- **Dark Theme**: Deep charcoal surfaces (`#171717`) perfect for late-night desk sessions.
- Dynamic color cues: **Tomato Coral** for Focus, **Calming Teal** for Short Breaks, and **Soft Blue** for Long Breaks.

### 6. 🔔 Gentle Notification Chimes
When your timer finishes, a calm, harmonic chime rings so you know when to step away from your screen. No harsh buzzer, no sudden jarring alarms.

### 7. 📊 Daily Goals & Automatic Date Rollover
- Set your personal daily target (e.g., 4 or 6 focus sessions per day).
- Live progress bar with motivational feedback.
- Manual `+` / `-` buttons to quickly log sessions if you studied away from your computer.
- Automatically resets your daily count when a new day arrives without losing your saved settings!

## 🧠 The Pomodoro Technique Explained

The technique was invented by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s and is named after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer he used in college (*pomodoro* is Italian for tomato).

```text
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ 1. Focus Sprint (25 - 60 min)                               │
  │    Choose one task. Zero distractions. Work with intention. │
  └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                 │
                                 ▼
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ 2. Short Break (5 - 10 min)                                 │
  │    Stand up. Stretch. Drink water. Rest your eyes.          │
  └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                 │  (Repeat 4 Times)
                                 ▼
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ 3. Long Break (15 - 30 min)                                 │
  │    Recharge fully. Go for a walk. Grab a snack.             │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 🛠️ Built for Developers & Portfolio

For recruiters, developers, and learners interested in clean web architecture:

- **100% Vanilla Code**: Zero external frameworks, npm packages, or build steps (No React, Tailwind, or Vue).
- **Accurate Timer Engine**: Uses timestamp difference (`Date.now() - endTime`) rather than standard `setInterval` ticks, ensuring countdowns stay 100% precise even when browser tabs are in the background or minimized.
- **Web Audio API**: Both ambient white/pink noise and musical bell chimes are synthesized algorithmically in the browser without requiring external `.mp3` or `.wav` files.
- **Local Storage Architecture**: Automatically saves and sanitizes user settings, daily counters, and custom times locally with date rollover checks.
- **Responsive & Accessible**: Uses semantic HTML5, SVG progress rings with smooth stroke-dashoffset transitions, ARIA announcements, and `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.
